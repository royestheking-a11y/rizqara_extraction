# Privacy Policy for Rizqara Extraction

**Effective Date:** May 14, 2026

Rizqara ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and discloses information.

### 1. Information We Collect
*   **User Account Information:** When you register/login, we collect your name and email address to manage your subscription and usage limits.
*   **Extracted Data:** The extension allows you to extract public business information from Google Maps (e.g., business name, phone number, website). This data is stored locally in your browser and synced to your private Rizqara dashboard.
*   **Technical Data:** We collect basic logs (IP address, browser version) to ensure the stability of our backend API.

### 2. How We Use Information
*   To provide the lead extraction and enrichment service.
*   To manage your daily usage limits based on your plan.
*   To provide customer support.

### 3. Data Disclosure & Third Parties
*   **We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your data** to third parties.
*   Your data is private to your account.
*   We only share data with third-party services (like OpenAI) if you explicitly enable the "AI Insights" feature, and only the specific business name/category is shared to generate insights.

### 4. Data Security
We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data transmissions between the extension and our servers.

### 5. User Control
You can delete your extracted leads at any time through the extension or by clearing your browser storage.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have questions, contact us at support@rizqara.com
